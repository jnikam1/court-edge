How to read this
  • Prices are Kalshi's. Each row shows the price to buy that side to win. Open a matchup for the sell price, the chart and the analysis.
  • Tennis scores come from Polymarket's live score and a point feed. NBA and NFL scores, injuries and the sportsbook line come from ESPN. The sportsbook number is DraftKings with its margin removed.
  • Price gaps: a tennis price that is off its usual level in the first minutes after a first set, or a game where Kalshi's buy price is 3¢ or more under the sportsbook after the fee. Tested on past games, both were fair on average, so treat a gap as a reason to look.
  • Injury watch covers NBA leading scorers for the next day and a half, and NFL starting quarterbacks and each team's lead runner and receiver for the next four days. The point values are a rough guide.
  • Nothing here is a proven edge or advice.

How to read this

  • Each whale is graded per category on its own full betting history, after fees. Strong means at least $50k profit, 1% return and 30 markets in that category. Weak means $50k or more lost.
  • A bet tagged with a strong category is that whale betting where it has actually made money. A weak tag is the same whale betting where it loses.
  • Big adds: a tracked whale bought $10k or more in the last 3 hours in a category it wins in, and now has $50k or more in that market. Buys under 5¢ or over 95¢ are left out.
  • Big adds show in the Alerts tab. The app checks every minute while it is open. For alerts with the app closed, turn on phone alerts there.
  • New marks bets opened since your last visit on this phone.
  • Kalshi hides positions unless a trader opts in. These are the profitable ones that did.
  • Past profit has not carried forward. Whales graded strong on their record up to August 1 then placed 603 one-sided bets of $50k or more in those categories. They won 61.2% at an average price of 60.6¢, which is break-even before fees. Many of these accounts also hedge elsewhere or make markets. Not advice.
